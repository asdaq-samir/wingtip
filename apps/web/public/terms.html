A planning aid, not for navigation

Wingtip Maps is a planning aid for VFR and IFR flight. It does not replace an official weather briefing (1800wxbrief.com or 1-800-WX-BRIEF), current FAA charts and publications, or the pilot in command’s judgement and final authority (14 CFR 91.3, 91.103). Its checkpoints are a model’s suggestions: check them against the chart. Before flight, verify current weather, NOTAMs, TFRs, airport status, aircraft performance, and applicable regulations (14 CFR 91.103).

The pilot in command

The pilot in command is directly responsible for, and is the final authority as to, the operation of the airplane (14 CFR 91.3), and must become familiar with all available information about a flight before beginning it (14 CFR 91.103). Nothing in the app changes that. Charts, weather, NOTAMs, airspace, altitudes, fuel, times and figures shown here may be late, incomplete or wrong; confirm each against its official source before you rely on it.

What the app is not

  • Not a collision-avoidance system. The traffic on the map comes from public ADS-B services, and from a cockpit receiver where you connect one. It is late, incomplete and shows only airplanes that broadcast; look outside and follow ATC.
  • Not a radio. The ATC copilot turns what your device hears into text with a speech recognizer, which mishears. A clearance is what the controller said, read back and confirmed on the frequency, never what the app wrote.
  • Not an official briefing, and it files no flight plan.
  • Not a logbook of record. Keep your own, and a copy of your data (Setup, Export My Data).

Words a model writes

Planning and flying with the app are free: the charts, the route, checkpoints, the nav log, altitudes, fuel, weather, airspace and the Go / No-Go. Where your phone has its own model (Apple Intelligence on an iPhone, Gemini Nano on Android), Ask and the words beside some figures are written by it on the phone. What a model writes can be wrong: check it as you would any briefing. What leaves your phone for them is in the Privacy policy.
