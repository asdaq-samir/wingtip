Support

Draft for the owner to review.

For help, a bug or a question, open an issue on our GitHub page: github.com/asdaq-samir/wingtip/issues.

Issues there are public, so do not put your email address, a tail number or anything else you want private in one.

Your data

  • A copy of it: Setup, under your account, Export My Data.
  • Deleting your account: in Wingtip Maps on your iPhone, Android phone or the web (wingtipmaps.app/app), Setup, under your account, Delete Account. It removes your account and everything we keep for it, on every device, at once. What that covers is in the Privacy policy.
  • Deleting your account without the app: go to wingtipmaps.app/delete-account and enter the email address you sign in with. We email that address a link; open it and press Delete Account. It deletes exactly what Setup's Delete Account does, and you need neither the app nor to sign in. The app is "Wingtip Maps" on the App Store and Google Play.

A security problem is not for a public issue: use GitHub's private vulnerability report on the same repository.

See also the Privacy policy and the Terms of use.
