# 0041. The server's Claude features are taken out

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Status:** Accepted. Supersedes [0035](0035-claude-off-until-subscription.md).

## Context

Since 2026-11-10 ([0035](0035-claude-off-until-subscription.md)) every
feature that asked Claude from the server had been off by default:
`APP_CLAUDE_FEATURES=OFF`, the `assistant` service started only with the
`claude` compose profile, and each app hiding what it could not offer.
The subscription that would pay for them is parked, the app is free
([0022](0022-free-app-for-now.md)), and nothing is in production, so the
switch, the service and every path behind it were code kept for a day
that has no date. Keeping them cost a service, an image, a CI leg, a
generated client on three apps and a gate on each screen that used one.

## Decision

The owner, 2026-11-12: "Take claude out."

The server makes no model call, and nothing in the product asks
Anthropic. Removed, with no flag or shim left behind:

- **The service:** `services/assistant` (the assistant, the Brief's
  narrative, the readings in words, the checkpoint notes' writing, the
  mock oral, a photographed page's reading), its image in
  `.github/images.hcl`, its compose service and the `claude` profile, its
  ECR repository in the CloudFormation template, and its CI leg
  (`test-assistant`).
- **The switches:** `APP_CLAUDE_FEATURES`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, the
  `ASSISTANT_*` model settings and `ASSISTANT_SERVICE_URL`, in both compose
  files, `.env.example`, `infra/` and the gateway's `AppProperties`.
- **The gateway:** its routes to the assistant (`AssistantApi`, the
  assistant half of `UpstreamRoutes`), the `claudeFeatures` capability, and
  the paid calls' daily allowances and monthly caps, which only Claude's
  features had. `PaidFeature` keeps the debrief alone.
- **The apps:** every screen and line that asked the assistant, the
  generated assistant clients (`assistant-schema.d.ts`, iOS's
  `AssistantAPI`, Android's `AssistantApi` and `AssistantModels`), and
  each app's Claude gate (`useClaudeOn`, `ServerFeatures.swift`,
  `ClaudeGate.kt`).
- **The engine contract's `"x-tool"` and `"x-assistant"` tags**, and the
  planner's internal `POST /api/engine/{name}` they allowed, which only
  the assistant asked.
- **The checkpoint notes' generation:** `POST /api/checkpoint-notes/seed`
  goes. The planner's read of a route's notes becomes
  `POST /api/checkpoint-notes/route`, forwarded by the gateway, so a
  pilot's own saved notes still load.

What stays:

- **On-device AI.** Apple's Foundation Models (and Private Cloud Compute
  where it is free) on the iPhone and Gemini Nano on Android: on-device
  Ask, the words over the weather, risk, NOTAMs, debrief and changes,
  and a photographed page read on the phone and checked by the engine.
- **The MCP connector** ([0036](0036-mcp-connector.md)): the pilot adds
  Wingtip to their own Claude or ChatGPT, and their subscription pays for
  the model. Its calls are generated by `scripts/build/planner_calls.py`
  (`planner-calls.sh`), which was the assistant client's generator.
- **The planner's own endpoints the assistant read**, such as
  `/api/words/{kind}/sources`, `/api/plan` and `/api/route-check`: they
  are the planner's API, and the connector or the apps may ask them.

## Consequences

- No Claude until there is a subscription to pay for it, and then it is a
  new decision, not a switch.
- The web has no Ask, words lines, written briefing, mock oral, trip
  planner from words or photo reading: no browser has a model the code
  uses. The phones keep what runs on the phone.
- The privacy pages, the store's privacy labels and the credits no longer
  name Anthropic as a recipient of the app's own features.
- **To restore it**, start from commit `96f0c80a9`, the last to change
  `services/assistant` (on `claude/integration`; `origin/main` has it as
  of `76a811833`), and bring back the service, its routes in the
  gateway, its generated clients and the screens listed above.
