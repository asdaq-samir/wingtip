# 0019. No explanatory footnotes on pilot screens

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Status:** Accepted

## Context

Pilot screens had collected explanatory footers and notes.

## Decision

"Avoid putting so many footnotes for pilots experience." Pilot-facing screens carry no explanatory footers or notes; only data and required credits stay. Reasoning is folded under its figure.

## Consequences

Put the why in a comment, a doc or a tap-to-open detail, not in a footer.

The owner, 2026-10-16.
