## How each conflict was resolved

- **The engine's .wasm** (geo-engine, engine-stock, notam-extras): a
  generated file, not merged by hand. Integration's side was taken each
  time, and after the last merge `scripts/build/build-engine-wasm.sh`
  built it from the merged engine (1,274,243 bytes, 553,142 gzipped).
- **simplicity_baseline.json** (simp-plan-workspace, simp-flight-session):
  the lower of the two counts for every entry (`web_plan_workspace_lines`
  76 from simp-plan-workspace; `ios_shared_references` 177,
  `ios_root_view_lines` 175, `android_map_pane_lines` 307,
  `android_map_pane_state_lines` 58, `android_composables_calling_engine`
  0, `ios_try_engine_default` 7 from simp-flight-session; the clone and
  one-home counts from integration), then `simplicity_ratchet.py --write`
  set them to the real counts (`files_over_400_lines` 132). No count rose.
- **Every other generated file** merged without a conflict and was
  generated again after the last merge: `cargo run -p wingtip-contract-gen`
  (367 rules), `web_rules.py`, `npm run types`, `engine-contract-apps.sh`,
  `assistant-client.sh`, `api-models-apps.sh` and the planner's
  `python -m app.openapi`. Only the .wasm and ci.yml's paths filter
  (`areas.py write`: simp-one-owner's `shots.schema.json` in the sim area)
  came out different.
- **The file-size lists**: `file_sizes.py --write` after the merge lowered
  six entries and dropped `engine/facade/src/call.rs` (1,171 to 70 lines,
  its arms now in the crates) and `notam_read/four.rs` from the new-file list.

  engine, and simp-plan-workspace moved usePlanWorkspace's straight-line
  distance into `features/plan/hooks/usePlanBrief.ts`. That memo now waits
