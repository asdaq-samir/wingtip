Thank you for flying Wingtip Maps before anyone else. It is a planning aid, not for navigation: check the chart, the NOTAMs and an official briefing before you fly, as you always do.

What to try, in whatever order suits you:

1. Plan a flight you know. Open FPL, type From and To (for example your home field and a field you fly to), and tap Plan. Are the checkpoints ones you would pick from the sectional? Is the cruise altitude one you would fly?
2. Read the nav log and the Brief. Are the headings, times, fuel and winds what your own plan gives? Is anything in the Brief wrong, missing or in the way?
3. IFR pilots: file-style routes with airways, a SID or STAR, an approach, and the alternate under IFR. Do the altitudes, the 1-2-3 alternate and the plates agree with yours?
4. Instructors: the logbook, the Coach and the instructor pages. Would you use them with a student?
5. Tap around the map: an airport's card (weather, runways, frequencies, charts), Nearest, an airspace, traffic, and the Map button's layers.
6. Take it flying as a passenger or in the right seat, never as your only reference: own ship, the alerts ahead, the Live Activity on the Lock Screen.
7. Download a region in Settings, Downloads, and try it in airplane mode.

Tell us when a figure is wrong. Say which flight, the figure, what you expected and where your figure comes from. A wrong figure is a safety problem, and we fix those first.

To send feedback: take a screenshot and tap Share Beta Feedback (TestFlight), or use Settings, About, Send Feedback. Feedback reaches only us.
