# The tester invitation (draft)

For the owner to send from his own address to each pilot, one at a time.
The pilots are the 5 to 10 of the beta plan ([../beta.md](../beta.md)),
a CFI and an IFR pilot among them. Fill in the brackets. Each store sends
its own invitation once the pilot is added (TestFlight's email or public
link, Play's opt-in link), so this mail says why and what to expect. The
words follow the app's: "airplane", a planning aid, no promises the app
does not keep.

---

**Subject:** Would you test a flight planner before it launches?

Hi [name],

I'm building Wingtip Maps, a flight planner for VFR and IFR pilots. It
puts the FAA chart on a map that works like Maps on a phone. It plans the
route, picks checkpoints off the sectional, and writes the nav log and a
briefing. Before it goes on the App Store and Google Play, I'd like a few
pilots I trust to fly it on paper and tell me what's wrong.

**What I'm asking:**

- Plan two or three flights you know well over the next four weeks, and
  check the checkpoints, the altitudes, the nav log and the Brief against
  your own planning.
- [For a CFI: look at the logbook, the Coach and the instructor pages
  with a student's eyes.] [For an IFR pilot: try airways, a SID or STAR,
  an approach and the alternate.]
- Tell me anything that's wrong, confusing or in your way. A wrong figure
  matters most: say which flight, the figure, what you expected and where
  yours comes from.

It's a planning aid, not for navigation. Keep using your charts, the
NOTAMs and an official briefing as you always do, and never fly it as
your only reference.

**How:**

- **iPhone:** you'll get an email from TestFlight. Install TestFlight from
  the App Store, then accept. [Or open this link on your iPhone:
  <TestFlight public link>.]
- **Android:** open <Play opt-in link> while signed in to Google Play with
  [the address you gave me], tap Become a Tester, then install Wingtip Maps
  from the Play Store.
- **Feedback:** in the app, Settings, About, Send Feedback, sent to me at
  <owner's address>. On an iPhone you can also take a screenshot and tap
  Share Beta Feedback. Or just reply to this email.

**What it collects:** if you sign in, the email you use (and the name
Apple gives, if you sign in with Apple), and what you choose to save:
airplanes, flights, logbook. Crash and speed reports carry the app's and
the phone's system version and nothing of yours. The privacy policy is at
<https://wingtipmaps.app/privacy>. You can delete your account in the
app at any time.

The beta is free, and so is the app for now. Thanks. It means a lot to
have real pilots look at it first.

[Owner's name]
