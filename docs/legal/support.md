# Support

*Draft for the owner to review.*

For help, a bug or a question, open an issue on our GitHub page: [github.com/asdaq-samir/wingtip/issues](https://github.com/asdaq-samir/wingtip/issues).

Issues there are public, so do not put your email address, a tail number or anything else you want private in one.

## Your data

- **A copy of it:** Setup, under your account, *Export My Data*.
- **Deleting your account:** Setup, under your account, *Delete Account*. It removes your account and everything we keep for it, on every device, at once. What that covers is in the [Privacy policy](/privacy).

A security problem is not for a public issue: use GitHub's private vulnerability report on the same repository.

See also the [Privacy policy](/privacy) and the [Terms of use](/terms).
